WOMEN'S HEALTH

Nutrition & Healthy Lifestyle

Learn how balanced nutrition, hydration, physical activity, sleep and healthy habits can support women's overall wellbeing.

Why Nutrition Matters

Good nutrition provides the body with energy and essential nutrients needed for growth, hormone function, immune health, bones and everyday activities.

Balanced Diet

A balanced diet includes a variety of foods from different food groups. Eating a wide range of nutritious foods can help provide the vitamins, minerals, protein, carbohydrates, fats and fiber the body needs.

Important Nutrients for Women

  • Iron – important for healthy blood and oxygen transport.
  • Calcium – supports strong bones and teeth.
  • Vitamin D – helps the body absorb calcium and supports bone health.
  • Protein – supports muscles, tissues and many body functions.
  • Fiber – supports healthy digestion.

Hydration

Drinking enough fluids is important for normal body functions. Water needs can vary depending on activity, climate, diet and individual health needs.

Healthy Food Choices

  • Fresh fruits and vegetables
  • Whole grains
  • Beans and lentils
  • Eggs, fish and other protein sources
  • Nuts and seeds
  • Milk and other nutritious dairy foods, when suitable

Physical Activity

Regular physical activity can support heart health, muscle strength, bone health, mood and overall wellbeing. Choose activities that are appropriate for your health and fitness level.

Sleep and Rest

Adequate sleep supports physical health, emotional wellbeing, concentration and daily functioning. Try to maintain a consistent sleep routine.

Managing Stress

Long-term stress can affect wellbeing and may influence sleep, mood, eating habits and other aspects of health. Relaxation, physical activity, supportive relationships and healthy routines may help.

Simple Healthy Habits

  • Eat a variety of nutritious foods.
  • Drink water regularly.
  • Stay physically active.
  • Get enough sleep.
  • Take time for relaxation.
  • Attend recommended health checkups.
Important: Nutrition needs are different for every person. If you have a medical condition, are pregnant, breastfeeding, or have specific dietary needs, discuss your diet with a qualified healthcare professional.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is iron important for women?

Iron is needed to make hemoglobin, a protein in red blood cells that helps carry oxygen throughout the body. Iron needs can be especially important for people who lose blood during menstruation.

Is drinking water important?

Yes. Adequate hydration supports normal body functions, although individual fluid needs can vary.

Does healthy eating mean avoiding all unhealthy foods?

Healthy eating is mainly about balance, variety and appropriate portions. Individual dietary needs can differ.
