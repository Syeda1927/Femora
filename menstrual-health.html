Menstrual Health

Understand periods, menstrual cycles, symptoms, period pain, PMS and menstrual wellbeing.

What is a Menstrual Cycle?

The menstrual cycle is a natural process involving hormonal changes in the reproductive system. Menstrual patterns can vary from person to person.

Common Period Symptoms

  • Lower abdominal cramps
  • Bloating
  • Breast tenderness
  • Mood changes
  • Fatigue
  • Headache

Period Pain

Some people experience cramps during menstruation. Mild discomfort can be common, but severe or worsening pain should be discussed with a healthcare professional.

Irregular Periods

Period timing can sometimes change because of stress, hormonal changes, weight changes, certain medications, pregnancy, PCOS and other health factors.

Heavy Menstrual Bleeding

Bleeding that is unusually heavy, lasts longer than expected, or interferes significantly with daily life should be evaluated by a healthcare professional.

PMS

Premenstrual syndrome may involve physical and emotional symptoms before a period, such as bloating, breast tenderness, mood changes and fatigue.

Self-Care Tips

  • Stay hydrated.
  • Eat balanced meals.
  • Get adequate sleep.
  • Use gentle physical activity if comfortable.
  • Track your cycle and symptoms.
When to seek medical advice: Severe pain, very heavy bleeding, fainting, persistent cycle changes or symptoms that interfere with daily life should be discussed with a qualified healthcare professional.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why can periods become irregular?

There can be many possible reasons, including hormonal changes, stress, pregnancy, PCOS and other conditions.

Should severe period pain be ignored?

No. Severe or persistent pain deserves professional evaluation.
