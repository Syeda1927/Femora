WOMEN'S HEALTH

Mental Health

Learn about emotional wellbeing, stress, anxiety, mood changes, self-care and when professional support may be helpful.

What is Mental Health?

Mental health is an important part of overall wellbeing. It can affect how we think, feel, manage stress, build relationships and handle everyday life.

Stress

Stress is a natural response to challenging situations. Short-term stress can sometimes help us respond to problems, but ongoing or overwhelming stress can affect wellbeing.

Common Signs of Stress

  • Feeling overwhelmed
  • Difficulty concentrating
  • Changes in sleep
  • Irritability
  • Tiredness
  • Changes in appetite

Anxiety

Anxiety can involve excessive worry, fear or nervousness. Occasional anxiety can happen to anyone, but persistent or severe anxiety may need professional evaluation.

Mood Changes

Mood can naturally change in response to life events, hormones, sleep and other factors. Significant or persistent mood changes should not be ignored.

Mental Health Self-Care

  • Maintain a regular sleep routine.
  • Stay physically active.
  • Spend time with supportive people.
  • Take breaks and allow yourself time to rest.
  • Talk to someone you trust when you are struggling.
  • Practice relaxing activities such as breathing exercises or gentle movement.

Women and Mental Wellbeing

Hormonal changes, reproductive health, pregnancy, postpartum changes, menopause, relationships, work and other life circumstances can all influence emotional wellbeing.

When Professional Support May Help

Consider talking to a qualified mental health professional if sadness, anxiety, stress, sleep problems or other emotional difficulties persist, become severe or interfere with daily life.

Important: If you feel unable to keep yourself safe or are experiencing an immediate mental health crisis, seek urgent professional help or contact local emergency services.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is stress always harmful?

No. Short-term stress can be a normal response to challenges. Problems may arise when stress becomes persistent or overwhelming.

Can sleep affect mental wellbeing?

Yes. Poor or insufficient sleep can affect mood, concentration, energy and emotional wellbeing.

Is asking for help a sign of weakness?

No. Seeking support when you need it is a healthy and responsible step.
